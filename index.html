Announcement

The 17th edition is on the press

Vietnam Lindy Exchange is an international swing dance weekend, held every year since 2009 — and in 2027 it returns to the UNESCO World Heritage town of Hội An. Three days and nights of swingin' live bands, international DJs and dancing by the beach and through the old town. Lindy Hop at heart, and loved by Balboa and Blues dancers from all over the world.

How registration works

  1. Fill in the registration form when a window opens.
  2. You'll get an acknowledgement email — don't pay yet.
  3. When a place is confirmed, we email you payment instructions.
  4. Pay by the deadline to secure your spot.

REGISTRATION

Group Registration

Opens Saturday 10 October · 16:00 (GMT+7)

  1. Your group leader fills in the group form when the window opens.
  2. If your group is among the first 10, we email you payment details.
  3. Complete payment within 24 hours to hold your places.
  4. The leader shares the individual form — every member submits it to confirm.

Groups of 6–10 dancers · maximum 10 groups.

Individual Registration

Opens Saturday 17 October · 16:00 (GMT+7)

  1. Fill in the individual form when the window opens.
  2. The first 50 dancers take the early-bird price.
  3. Watch your inbox for payment instructions, then pay within 24 hours.
  4. Your place is confirmed once we receive your payment.

Regular registration opens 24 October — or earlier if the early bird sells out.

Questions & answers

The Reader Asks

When and where is VLX 2027?

Friday 19 – Sunday 21 March 2027, in Hội An, Vietnam. The exact venues for each dance are being confirmed and will be published here as soon as they are locked in.

When does registration open, and how much is a pass?

There are three windows, opening on these dates right here on this website: group registration on 10 October 2026 (groups of 6–10 dancers, maximum 10 groups), early bird on 17 October 2026 (the first 50 registrations get the early bird price), and regular registration on 24 October 2026. Exact opening times and prices will be updated here along the way.

Which dance styles can I expect?

Mainly Lindy Hop, with dedicated late-night sessions loved by Balboa and Blues dancers. DJs and live bands play across all three nights.

How do I get there?

Fly into Da Nang International Airport (DAD), roughly a 40-minute drive from Hội An. We will share ride-coordination tips and private-car options closer to the event.

Where should I stay?

We are finalising accommodation details with our venue partners and will publish options and any special rates here once they are confirmed.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full terms — including our pass-transfer policy — will be published before registration opens. In short: VLX does not offer refunds, but passes can be transferred to another dancer within the stated deadline.
