The essentials

What
Vietnam Lindy Exchange (VLX) — an international swing dance weekend: live bands, international DJs, a gala dinner, beach and old-town dancing, and late-night sessions.
Styles
Mainly Lindy Hop, also loved by Balboa and Blues dancers.
Where
Hội An, Vietnam — a UNESCO World Heritage town.
When
The 17th edition returns in 2027. Exact dates to be announced on 19 September 2026.
Since
Held annually since 2009.
