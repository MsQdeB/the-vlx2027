“Many happy feet since 2009” — The Vietnam Lindy Exchange
Announcement

The 17th edition is on the press

Vietnam Lindy Exchange is an international swing dance weekend held annually since 2009. In recent years, it takes place in the UNESCO World Heritage town of Hoi An, Central Vietnam. Expect swingin' live bands, international DJs, a gala dinner, and dancing by the beach and through the old town. While mainly a Lindy Hop event, VLX is also loved by Balboa and Blues dancers for its late-night sessions and warm, welcoming vibe that draws dancers from all over the world.

We are thrilled to announce Vietnam Lindy Exchange's 17th edition.
March 19–20–21, 2027
Hội An, Vietnam

Mark your calendar and get ready for another unforgettable weekend filled with
great dances, live music, amazing food and many happy feet.

TRANSPORTATION BETWEEN DA NANG AIRPORT & HOI AN

The closest airport to the VLX event is the Da Nang International Airport - DAD. The VLX participants are responsible for making their own way to their hotel from the airport. It is roughly a 40 minutes drive to most places in Hoi An. You can use our Facebook Event if you wish to share a ride together with some other dancers with similar arrival time. Grab Car & Xanh SM are also available at the airport.

VLXers can also book private car with Mr Hai:

  • 4 seaters: 250,000 VND per trip (2 people)
  • 7 seaters: 300,000 VND per trip (max 4 people with luggage)
  • 16-seat van: 400,000 VND per trip (max 10 people with luggage)
  • Before 6am and after 10pm pick up: add 50,000 VND/trip

Email: honghainguyen143@gmail.com. Mobile: +84 (0)919 433 201

Questions & answers

The Reader Asks

When and where is VLX 2027?

Friday 19 – Sunday 21 March 2027, in Hội An, Vietnam. The main evening dances will be held at Palm Garden Beach Resort & Spa, with daytime events in town and by the beach.

When does registration open, and how much is a pass?

There are three windows, opening on these dates right here on this website: group registration on 10 October 2026 (groups of 6–10 dancers, maximum 10 groups), early bird on 17 October 2026 (the first 50 registrations get the early bird price), and regular registration on 24 October 2026. Exact opening times and prices will be updated here along the way.

Will there be day passes?

Yes — a limited number of day passes for individual nights will be available once registration opens. Full weekend passes are prioritised.

What dance levels is VLX for?

VLX is a social event for intermediate and above social dancers — there are no classes or taster sessions, so you should feel comfortable dancing socially. If you are unsure, email us and we will help you decide.

Which dance styles can I expect?

Mainly Lindy Hop, with dedicated late-night sessions loved by Balboa and Blues dancers. DJs and live bands play across all three nights.

How do I get there?

Fly into Da Nang International Airport (DAD), then take a 40-minute car ride to Hội An. We will share ride-coordination tips and private-car options closer to the event — see the Transportation section on this page.

Where should I stay?

Palm Garden Beach Resort hosts all main evening dances and offers special rates for VLXers. Alternative accommodation near the venue is listed in the Accommodation section once details are confirmed.

What is the cancellation policy?

VLX does not offer refunds, but passes can be transferred to another dancer — see the Terms page for the full policy and deadlines.
