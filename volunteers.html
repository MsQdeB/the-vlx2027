VLX 2027 - Volunteer Application

We're Looking for 3 Core Volunteers

Vietnam Lindy Exchange is built on community, care, and shared responsibility. This year, we're opening a clear and intentional call for 3 on-site volunteers who want to be part of the heart of VLX, not just helping out, but running it together with us.

🤝 What this role is about

You'll be one of the faces of VLX throughout the event.

This is an active, all-day role, from morning to night, including:

  • Helping with check-in and registration.
  • Supporting coordination between dancers, bands, DJs, venues, and organizers.
  • Communicating with local partners and service providers.
  • Keeping an eye on the flow of the event and stepping in when help is needed.
  • Supporting dancers and guests with clarity, warmth, and awareness. Being present for every swing out in between ✨

This is not a stand-by or "free-pass" role. It requires responsibility, initiative, and care. Of course, we have fun and swing out, too.

💛 Who we're looking for

We're looking for people who:

  • Have strong team spirit and enjoy working closely with others
  • Are reliable, proactive, and observant
  • Can stay calm, friendly, and solution-oriented when things get busy
  • Care deeply about community and shared experience

🌍 Language requirements

Because this role involves working closely with both international guests and local partners, volunteers must:

  • Be fluent in Vietnamese (required)
  • Be proficient in English (required)
  • Knowledge of other languages is a plus

🎁 What you receive

  • Free full VLX pass
  • Free accommodation for all 3 days of the event
  • A chance to be part of the core organizing team
  • Extra meal stipend 💫

🌱 Our spirit

We want volunteers who feel:

"This is our event, and I'm here to help it be the best it can be."

📩 How to apply

Please fill out our short volunteer application form.

We'll review applications and contact selected candidates for a short chat.

Spots are limited to 3 volunteers.

Please read the questions carefully and answer honestly. This helps us build a team with shared expectations and spirit.
